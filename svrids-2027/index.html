Academic & Venture Pathways

Six Specialized DeepTech Tracks

Explore our multidisciplinary tracks covering the entire innovation spectrum—from discovery and patent protection to enterprise scaling and venture financing.

01
Track 1 • Commercialization

DeepTech Ventures & Commercialization

Overcoming the lab-to-market chasm, spinout incubation frameworks, prototype de-risking, and scale-up manufacturing economics.

  • Lab bench to market acceleration
  • Spinout incubation & university hubs
  • Scale-up dynamics & pilot deployment
  • Hard-tech manufacturing & supply chain
02
Track 2 • Tech Transfer

Research-to-Startup Pathways

Academic founder journeys, university Tech Transfer Office (TTO) best practices, proof-of-concept grant blending, and spinout equity structures.

  • Faculty & PhD founder roadmaps
  • University TTO licensing frameworks
  • Proof-of-concept (PoC) funding mechanisms
  • Conflict-of-interest & dual-role governance
03
Track 3 • AI Ecosystem

AI & ML Startup Ecosystem

Vertical AI agent systems, specialized foundational model fine-tuning, generative enterprise tools, and hardware acceleration infrastructure.

  • Autonomous AI agent workflows
  • Enterprise LLM orchestration & RAG
  • Silicon, edge computing & GPU clusters
  • AI-driven synthetic data & compliance
04
Track 4 • IP & Patents

IP Strategy & Patent Commercialization

Strategic patent drafting, global defense, Freedom-to-Operate (FTO) analyses, patent pools, and royalty monetization architectures.

  • Global patent portfolio construction
  • Freedom-to-operate & prior art landscaping
  • Technology licensing & valuation models
  • Trade secret vs patent trade-offs
05
Track 5 • Investment

Funding, Investment & Pitch Readiness

Demystifying deep-tech term sheets, non-dilutive government grant blending, angel syndication, and preparing academic founders for Series A.

  • DeepTech VC term sheet analysis
  • Non-dilutive grant & equity co-financing
  • Valuation models for pre-revenue deeptech
  • Due diligence & cap table optimization
06
Track 6 • Sustainability

Social Impact Innovation & SDGs

Deep-tech innovations addressing climate decarbonization, accessible healthcare devices, clean water, circular materials, and UN SDGs.

  • Decarbonization & carbon capture tech
  • Affordable point-of-care medical devices
  • Circular economy & waste valorization
  • Inclusive technology & emerging market models
