Summit Overview

About SVRIDS 2027

Accelerating the journey from fundamental scientific breakthrough to market-ready deep-tech enterprises, venture scaling, and real-world socioeconomic impact.

The Core Problem

Bridging the DeepTech Valley of Death

Globally, billions of dollars are invested in academic research, resulting in profound technological discoveries across AI, quantum systems, photonics, biotechnology, and clean energy. Yet, over 90% of university inventions stall before reaching commercial viability.

SVRIDS 2027 was created to dismantle the structural barriers between academic labs and the venture ecosystem. By uniting principal investigators, tech transfer officers, corporate R&D heads, and deep-tech venture capitalists, we provide a collaborative launchpad for laboratory spinouts, patent monetization, and founder acceleration.

Translational Rigor

Pairing rigorous peer-reviewed academic validation with commercial proof-of-concept roadmaps.

Venture Readiness

Preparing academic founders with cap table structuring, term sheet literacy, and investor pitch skills.

Summit Governance

Our Guiding Principles

  • Zero Predatory Practices: Strict peer review, transparent fee schedules, and registered ISBN publication.
  • Dual-Track Evaluation: Academic contributions evaluated by scholars; venture pitches evaluated by active venture capitalists.
  • Inclusive Global Access: Hybrid architecture ensuring high-definition remote participation alongside physical networking.
  • Long-Term Incubation: Post-summit mentorship, investor introductions, and tech transfer whitepapers.

Participant Ecosystem

Who Should Attend SVRIDS 2027

A multidimensional gathering bringing together key stakeholders across the innovation and investment pipeline.

Academic Researchers & Lab Leads

Professors, principal investigators, and postdoctoral scholars seeking to commercialize their patented inventions and explore spinout pathways.

DeepTech Founders & Entrepreneurs

Early-stage hard-tech and AI founders looking to pitch to specialized deeptech venture capitalists, recruit technical co-founders, and secure pilot partners.

University Tech Transfer Officers (TTOs)

Directors and licensing managers navigating university spinout equity models, royalty frameworks, and cross-border IP monetization strategies.

Venture Capitalists & Angel Syndicates

Seed-to-Series A investors and family offices seeking early, proprietary access to high-defensibility laboratory spinouts and frontier IP.

Corporate R&D & Open Innovation Leaders

Industry executives scouting disruptive university technologies, sponsoring collaborative research, and establishing strategic licensing pacts.

PhD Scholars & Early-Career Innovators

Graduate students and research fellows aiming to transition from academic publishing to venture building, intellectual property, and industrial impact.
