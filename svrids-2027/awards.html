Distinction Categories

2027 Award Categories

All accepted presentations and pitch arena showcases are automatically considered by our awards jury.

Grand Prize • $5,000 Grant

Best DeepTech Venture Award

Presented to the most compelling laboratory spinout or early-stage startup demonstrating technical defensibility, massive market opportunity, and clear execution capability.

  • $5,000 Non-dilutive grant
  • VIP pitch slot to apex investor syndicate
  • Commemorative physical crystal trophy
Research Excellence

Outstanding Research-to-Impact Paper

Awarded to the academic paper exhibiting the highest level of methodological rigor, technological novelty, and immediate translational applicability.

  • Featured plenary presentation slot
  • Publication spotlight in proceedings
  • Certificate of Excellence
Early-Career

Young Founder & Innovator Award

Recognizing outstanding entrepreneurial achievement by a student, PhD candidate, or researcher under 35 years old driving frontier commercialization.

  • 1-on-1 mentorship with venture partners
  • Complimentary pass to SVRIDS 2028
  • Global press feature
Tech Transfer

Best University Spinout Showcase

Honoring the most successful university technology transfer office and founding faculty collaboration in licensing and incubating deep-tech IP.

  • Institutional recognition citation
  • TTO digest case study publication
  • Licensing roundtable spotlight
Sustainability

Social Impact DeepTech Award

Presented to innovations utilizing deep technologies to solve pressing UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), clean climate, or accessible healthcare.

  • Impact syndicate intro
  • SDG citation medal
  • Global media feature
Track Honors

Best Presentation Awards (Per Track)

Conferred upon the top oral and virtual presentation in each of the 6 individual summit tracks as evaluated by session chairs and attendee ballots.

  • Individual Track Certificate
  • Highlighted badge in digital proceedings
