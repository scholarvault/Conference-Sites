Leadership Structure

Summit Governance Pillars

Technical Program Committee (TPC)

Oversees double-blind peer review, plagiarism screening, track taxonomy integrity, and proceedings editorial standards for all academic contributions.

Active venture capitalists, angel syndicate leads, and serial deeptech founders reviewing executive pitch summaries and judging live arena presentations.

Reviewer Network

Committee Responsibilities & Reviewer Standards

SVRIDS 2027 committee members and peer reviewers are committed to academic rigor, prompt evaluation turnaround (3–5 working days), and constructive feedback.

Objective Peer Review: Evaluate manuscripts on technical novelty, experimental methodology, and reproducibility.
Commercial Feasibility Screening: Assess market size, manufacturing bottlenecks, IP moats, and founder defensibility for pitch decks.
Official Recognition & Citation: All committee members and active reviewers are formally credited in the published ISBN proceedings volume.
