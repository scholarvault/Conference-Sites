Call for Papers, Pitches & Whitepapers

Submit your original scientific papers, deeptech founder pitch decks, or university tech transfer case studies. All submissions undergo double-blind peer review and venture assessment within 3–5 working days.

Track Taxonomy

Six Research & Commercialization Streams

Authors and founders may submit under any of the six official conference tracks outlined below.

01
Track 1

DeepTech Ventures & Commercialization

Overcoming technological and market risks in lab-to-market translation, university incubators, pilot plant economics, and hard-tech manufacturing.

  • Technological readiness level (TRL) escalation
  • Hard-tech prototype de-risking & scale-up
  • Spinout incubation & university co-creation
  • Supply chain localization & fabrication models
  • Techno-economic feasibility modeling
02
Track 2

Research-to-Startup Pathways

Institutional roadmaps for academic founders, university TTO governance, conflict-of-interest policies, and non-dilutive proof-of-concept grant structures.

  • Academic inventor to venture CEO roadmaps
  • University equity vs royalty licensing models
  • Proof-of-concept (PoC) grant blending
  • Dual-role academic entrepreneurship policies
  • Collaborative university-industry consortia
03
Track 3

AI & ML Startup Ecosystem

Frontier generative models, autonomous multi-agent systems, domain-specific foundation models, edge AI hardware, and enterprise AI commercialization.

  • Vertical AI agents & autonomous workflows
  • Specialized LLM fine-tuning & enterprise RAG
  • Hardware accelerators, GPUs & edge silicon
  • AI data governance & synthetic benchmark testing
  • Defensibility & moat engineering for AI startups
04
Track 4

IP Strategy & Patent Commercialization

Strategic patent prosecution, freedom-to-operate landscaping, patent pooling, valuation of early-stage IP, and international patent enforcement.

  • Global patent portfolio construction
  • Freedom-to-operate (FTO) & prior art analytics
  • IP valuation methods for pre-revenue deeptech
  • Standard essential patents (SEPs) & licensing pools
  • Trade secrets vs patents strategic frameworks
05
Track 5

Funding, Investment & Pitch Readiness

Navigating venture capital term sheets, angel syndicates, grant-equity blending, cap table hygiene for university spinouts, and preparing for Series A.

  • DeepTech VC term sheets & valuation norms
  • Non-dilutive grant & venture equity blending
  • Cap table structures & university spinout equity
  • Due diligence readiness & data room audits
  • Milestone-based venture debt & financing
06
Track 6

Social Impact Innovation & SDGs

Deep-tech innovations designed for global sustainability, climate resilience, carbon capture, accessible med-tech devices, clean water, and inclusive tech.

  • Decarbonization, carbon capture & clean tech
  • Point-of-care medical diagnostics & bio-devices
  • Circular materials & industrial waste valorization
  • Inclusive digital infrastructure & emerging market models
  • UN Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) metrics
Contribution Types

Eligible Submission Formats

SVRIDS 2027 welcomes three distinct formats catering to researchers, founders, and tech transfer professionals.

1. Research Paper Track

Original academic papers detailing experimental discoveries, theoretical frameworks, or empirical techno-economic analyses. Published in ISBN proceedings.

  • 250–500 word abstract initially
  • Full manuscript 6–10 pages for proceedings
  • Blind peer-review by academic domain chairs

2. Startup Pitch Deck Track

Executive pitch summaries and 10–12 slide pitch decks from laboratory spinouts or deep-tech founders seeking venture capital and pilot partners.

  • Executive summary (300 words)
  • PDF pitch deck upload
  • Evaluated by active venture capitalists

3. Tech Transfer Whitepapers

Case studies and policy whitepapers from university TTO directors, legal counsels, and incubators sharing institutional commercialization models.

  • Abstract & institutional summary
  • Full whitepaper 4–8 pages
  • Featured in SVRIDS TTO Best Practice Digest
Academic & Venture Integrity

Evaluation Standards & Rapid Quality Review

ScholarVault adheres to rigorous double-blind peer review and venture assessment standards. We operate on a rolling evaluation schedule, delivering formal decisions within 3 to 5 working days.

Originality & Plagiarism Standard

All academic manuscripts undergo similarity screening (<15% threshold). Commercial pitches must disclose institutional IP rights and founder affiliations.

Constructive Review Feedback

Reviewers provide actionable feedback on technical defensibility, commercial market size, regulatory pathways, and presentation clarity.

Ready to Submit?

Submission Checklist

  • Selected relevant Track (1 through 6)
  • Author / Founder names and affiliations
  • PDF/DOCX file uploaded for review
  • No submission fee required at initial intake
