A

Abstract submission

Submit original work aligned to one or more summit tracks. Abstract submission is currently free.

R

Review window

Abstracts are evaluated by the designated academic review team. Typical decisions take 2-4 working days.

V

Virtual delivery

Accepted presenters receive session and technical instructions before the summit.

C

Official credentials

Applicable presentation or participation credentials depend on verified role and attendance records.

Submission essentials

Prepare

Title, abstract, keywords, author details, institutional affiliation and selected research track.

Keep transparent

Disclose relevant AI assistance, conflicts, authorship contributions and ethical considerations where applicable.

Publication & indexing transparency: Acceptance or presentation does not guarantee journal publication, indexing, DOI, ISBN or inclusion in a particular archive. Final routes and identifiers will be disclosed only when confirmed.
