Summit Overview

Setting the Benchmark for Responsible AI

As generative AI transforms academic publication, peer review, scientific discovery, and automated decision-making, SVRIAS 2026 convenes global scholars and industry leaders to safeguard truth, transparency, and research integrity.

01 / INTEGRITY CRISIS

Safeguarding Science

Addressing synthetic paper-mills, hallucinatory citations, and automated image tampering through forensic detection algorithms and robust verification standards.

  • Deepfake figure forensic detection
  • Paper-mill disruption methodologies
02 / GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORKS

Regulatory & Policy Alignment

Operationalizing the EU AI Act, international safety treaties, algorithmic auditing protocols, and institutional compliance standards for machine intelligence.

  • Auditing autonomous decision systems
  • Human-in-the-loop governance
03 / REPRODUCIBLE SCIENCE

Artifacts & Open Evidence

Transitioning academic publishing toward verifiable artifacts, containerized computational reproduction, and transparent author contribution taxonomies (CRediT).

  • Verifiable computational benchmarks
  • AI co-authorship attribution norms
Research Scope

Six Pillars of AI Governance & Integrity

Submit original theoretical, empirical, algorithmic, or policy contributions under our six thematic tracks.

TRACK 01

AI Ethics & Governance

Algorithmic bias mitigation, safety benchmarks, international compliance treaties, and human-in-the-loop oversight frameworks.

  • Algorithmic fairness & debiasing
  • Regulatory policy & EU AI Act
  • Autonomous decision accountability
Track Guidelines
TRACK 02

Research Integrity in the AI Era

Detecting synthetic data fabrication, automated image tampering, LLM hallucinations, and maintaining publication standards.

  • Forensic synthetic image analysis
  • Hallucination audits in literature
  • Anti-predatory publishing defenses
Track Guidelines
TRACK 03

Responsible AI Frameworks

Explainable AI (XAI), model interpretability, robust adversarial red-teaming, and safety verification across critical domains.

  • Explainable & interpretable ML
  • Adversarial robustness & red-teaming
  • Value alignment & constitutional AI
Track Guidelines
TRACK 04

Authorship, Attribution & AI Content

Establishing rigorous co-authorship boundaries, licensing generative outputs, cryptographic watermarking, and provenance norms.

  • AI contributor taxonomies & CRediT
  • Watermarking & provenance tracking
  • Training data copyright and consent
Track Guidelines
TRACK 05

Peer Review & Reproducibility

Open code and artifact sharing, algorithmic peer-review auditing, benchmark reproducibility, and verification standards.

  • Artifact badges & container verification
  • AI-assisted peer-review integrity
  • Reproducibility crisis interventions
Track Guidelines
TRACK 06

Data Privacy & Accountability

Differential privacy, secure federated learning, synthetic data ethics, model auditing, and machine unlearning boundaries.

  • Differential privacy & federated ML
  • Synthetic data privacy boundaries
  • Third-party algorithmic auditing
