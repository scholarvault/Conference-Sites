TRACK 01

AI Ethics & Governance

Focuses on ethical frameworks, human rights impacts, safety alignment, bias quantification, and regulatory statutes such as the EU AI Act.

  • Algorithmic bias mitigation & fair scoring
  • Regulatory compliance & legal liability frameworks
  • Autonomous agent supervision & oversight
  • AI ethics board governance in academia
Submit to Track 1
TRACK 02

Research Integrity in the AI Era

Forensic tools to combat scientific fraud, synthetic paper mills, hallucinatory citations, and manipulated experimental figures.

  • Deepfake scientific image forensic detection
  • Automated detection of LLM-generated manuscripts
  • Paper-mill disruption & citation cartels
  • Anti-predatory publishing verification
Submit to Track 2
TRACK 03

Responsible AI Frameworks

Explainable AI (XAI), Mechanistic Interpretability, adversarial red-teaming, constitutional AI, and safety verification across high-stakes domains.

  • Mechanistic interpretability for foundation LLMs
  • Adversarial attack simulation & red-teaming
  • Uncertainty quantification in automated clinical ML
  • Value alignment & constitutional safeguards
Submit to Track 3
TRACK 04

Authorship, Attribution & AI Content

Establishing rigorous co-authorship criteria, adapting the CRediT taxonomy, cryptographic model watermarking, and IP governance.

  • Defining non-human contributions in scholarly work
  • Statistical & cryptographic watermarking of text
  • Training data provenance & copyright infringement
  • Transparent disclosure frameworks for academic journals
Submit to Track 4
TRACK 05

Peer Review & Reproducibility

Open science ecosystems, containerized code verification, automated peer-review auditing, and resolving the computational reproducibility crisis.

  • Artifact badging & containerized code audits
  • Mitigating bias in AI-assisted peer evaluation
  • Open dataset licensing and validation protocols
  • Pre-registration of machine learning experiments
Submit to Track 5
TRACK 06

Data Privacy & Accountability

Differential privacy, secure multi-party computation, federated learning privacy guarantees, synthetic data ethics, and machine unlearning.

  • Differential privacy in distributed AI
  • Synthetic data fidelity vs. privacy preservation
  • Machine unlearning & the right to be forgotten
  • Third-party algorithmic auditing & compliance
Submit to Track 6
AUTHOR GUIDELINES

Submission & Review Instructions

  • Abstract Word Count: Structured abstracts must range between 250 and 500 words, including background, objective, methodology, results, and ethical implications.
  • Review Turnaround (SLA): Rolling reviews are processed by the technical editorial desk within 2 to 4 working days of submission.
  • Universal Format: All accepted abstracts are designated as official "Virtual Research Presentations" with equal recognition.
  • Archival Proceedings: Accepted presentations will be published in the SVRIAS 2026 Proceedings with an ISBN and digital permanent link.
  • Integrity Disclosures: Authors must state whether generative AI tools were utilized in the preparation of the manuscript, following Track 4 guidelines.
