Academic Honors

Conference Awards & Recognition

Honoring outstanding scholarship, exceptional artifact transparency, forensic integrity breakthroughs, and peer-review service at SVRIAS 2026.

Award Categories

Recognizing Scientific Excellence

Eligible accepted contributions may be considered for SVRIAS 2026 awards. Award criteria, evaluation procedures, jury composition and conflict-of-interest safeguards will be published before the award evaluation process begins.

Grand Prize

Best Paper in AI Ethics & Governance

Awarded to the paper demonstrating the highest theoretical novelty, policy impact, and rigorous empirical validation of algorithmic fairness frameworks.

Open Science Prize

Transparency & Reproducibility Award

Recognizes the contribution with the most complete, verified, and easily reproducible code/data artifact ecosystem.

Presentation Honor

Best Virtual Research Presentation

Honors the presenter delivering the most engaging, clear, and pedagogically effective virtual research presentation during live summit stages.

Early Career

Outstanding Young Researcher Award

Conferred on exceptional PhD candidates or postdocs within 3 years of degree completion who exhibit transformative research leadership.

Forensic Innovation

Forensic Detection Innovation Award

Awarded for pioneering methods in detecting synthetic paper-mill artifacts, image tampering, or deepfake scientific figure anomalies.

Service Award

Distinguished Reviewer Service Award

Acknowledging peer reviewers who deliver exemplary, thorough, and highly constructive evaluations during rapid review cycles.
