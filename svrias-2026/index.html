Research Scope

Six Pillars of AI Governance & Integrity

Explore our six thematic tracks spanning foundational ethics, reproducibility frameworks, data privacy, and attribution norms.

01

AI Ethics & Governance

Algorithmic bias mitigation, safety benchmarks, international AI compliance treaties, and human-in-the-loop oversight frameworks.

  • Algorithmic fairness & debiasing
  • Regulatory policy & EU AI Act alignment
  • Autonomous decision transparency
02

Research Integrity in the AI Era

Detecting synthetic data fabrication, automated image tampering, LLM hallucinations, and maintaining publication standards.

  • Deepfake scientific figure forensic detection
  • Hallucination audit in literature reviews
  • Anti-predatory publishing defenses
03

Responsible AI Frameworks

Explainable AI (XAI), model interpretability, robust red-teaming methodologies, and safety verification across critical domains.

  • Explainable & interpretable ML models
  • Adversarial robustness & red-teaming
  • Value alignment & constitutional AI
04

Authorship, Attribution & AI Content

Establishing rigorous co-authorship boundaries, licensing generative outputs, watermarking, and proper attribution standards.

  • AI co-authorship & contributor taxonomies
  • Watermarking & provenance tracking
  • Copyright and training data provenance
05

Peer Review & Reproducibility

Open code and artifact sharing, algorithmic peer-review auditing, benchmark reproducibility, and verification standards.

  • Artifact badges & code verification
  • AI-assisted peer-review integrity
  • Reproducibility crisis interventions
06

Data Privacy & Accountability

Differential privacy, secure multi-party computation, synthetic data ethics, model auditing, and algorithmic accountability.

  • Differential privacy & federated learning
  • Synthetic data privacy preservation
  • Third-party algorithmic auditing

Answers to Common Questions

Everything you need to know about submissions, presentation format, and registration.

All abstract submissions undergo rapid editorial review by our technical committee within 2 to 4 working days of submission. You will receive an official decision letter via email.
SVRIAS 2026 is a 100% virtual conference conducted via Zoom. All presentation sessions, keynote addresses, and networking breakout rooms take place online.
Every accepted contribution is designated as an official "Virtual Research Presentation". We do not differentiate between oral and poster tiers, ensuring equal recognition.
