⚠️ Important Warning: Unregulated National Conferences

The term "National Conference" is not regulated in India. Anyone can create a website, call it a "National Conference," and start collecting registration fees. There is no government approval or certification required. This means the onus of verification falls entirely on the researcher.

The "National Conference" Problem in India

Search for "national conference 2026 India" on Google and you'll find hundreds of results. Many look professional. Many claim Scopus indexing. Many have registration forms ready to accept your money. But a significant number of these are conference mills — operations that churn out events across multiple cities, collecting fees while providing minimal academic value.

These operations typically:

  • Run the same "national conference" brand in 10+ cities simultaneously
  • Accept papers from every discipline (engineering, medicine, management, arts, law) in a single event
  • Promise same-day certificates and "best paper awards" to most attendees
  • Claim Scopus or Web of Science indexing that cannot be independently verified
  • Charge ₹5,000–₹30,000 registration fees with no refund policy

City-Wise Conference Landscape

📍 Chennai

Chennai is home to IIT Madras, Anna University, SRM, VIT Chennai, and Loyola College — all of which organise legitimate national conferences. However, Chennai also hosts numerous predatory conference operators who leverage the city's academic density. When searching for "national conference 2026 Chennai," verify that the organiser is one of these established institutions or an affiliated department.

📍 Delhi / NCR

Delhi NCR hosts conferences by IIT Delhi, JNU, Delhi University, IIIT Delhi, and Amity. The National Capital Region sees heavy predatory conference activity due to its central location and high researcher population. Be especially cautious of "national conferences" at unnamed "5-star hotels" in Delhi.

📍 Bangalore

Bangalore's tech ecosystem includes conferences by IISc, IIM Bangalore, IIIT Bangalore, and PES University. IEEE Bangalore Section also organises legitimate events. Conference mills operate here too, often targeting IT and management researchers.

📍 Hyderabad, Pune, Kolkata

These cities host quality conferences through IITs, NITs, IIIT Hyderabad, Pune University, Jadavpur University, and others. Apply the same verification steps regardless of city. Location is not a guarantee of legitimacy.

How to Find Legitimate National Conferences

  1. Check your department's website: University departments often share CFPs for conferences they endorse or are affiliated with.
  2. Professional society websites: IEEE India (ieeeindiacouncil.org), CSI India (csi-india.org), and discipline-specific societies list verified events.
  3. Ask your supervisor: Guide professors and lab seniors have institutional knowledge about which conferences are credible in your field.
  4. Check DBLP, DOAJ, and CORE Rankings: Verify if the conference or its proceedings appear on DBLP, in the DOAJ (for associated journals), or the CORE Conference Rankings (825+ ranked conferences). Also check against Kscien's predatory conference database (~450 identified predatory venues).
  5. Verify with ScholarVault: Paste the conference URL into app.scholarvault.in for an instant 18-point forensic audit and Trust Score. Our engine cross-references DOAJ, CORE Rankings, UGC-CARE lists, Kscien databases, Retraction Watch, Beall's List, and DBLP for comprehensive verification.

What to Do If You've Already Registered at a Suspicious Conference

  • Don't present your paper. Publishing at a predatory conference is worse than not publishing at all.
  • Request a refund. If you paid via credit card or UPI, contact your bank for a chargeback if the organiser refuses.
  • Report it. Forward details to your university's research integrity cell or IQAC (Internal Quality Assurance Cell).
  • Don't list it on your CV. Remove the conference from your academic profile to avoid scrutiny during evaluations.
