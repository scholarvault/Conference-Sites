Registration & Refund Policy

Transparent, fair cancellation terms, full organizer postponement guarantees, and flexible delegate substitution protocols.

1. Transparency Principle

Under the ScholarVault Platinum Conference Charter, all registration fees are transparently published without hidden surcharges. We maintain a fair cancellation framework recognizing unforeseen research scheduling conflicts.

2. Event Postponement / Cancellation Guarantee

In the unlikely event that ICBBSB 2026 is cancelled or indefinitely postponed by the organizing committee:

  • All registered delegates and presenting authors will receive a 100% full refund of their registration fee within 7 business days.
  • Alternatively, delegates may choose to carry forward their registration pass to the subsequent edition with an additional 15% credit voucher.

3. Author & Delegate Cancellation Schedule

If an author or attendee must withdraw their registration, refunds are processed according to the following timeline:

Greater than 30 Days Prior (Before 18 Nov 2026) Early cancellation window
100% Refund (less 5% bank charge)
15 to 30 Days Prior (18 Nov – 03 Dec 2026) Mid-stage schedule adjustment window
50% Refund
Less than 15 Days Prior (After 03 Dec 2026) Final programme printing & proceedings production phase
Non-refundable

4. Delegate Substitution Policy (Free of Charge)

If a presenting author or registered attendee is unable to join the live virtual broadcast, they may nominate a co-author or institutional colleague to present in their place at zero additional administrative cost.

Substitution notices must be submitted via email to conferences@scholarvault.in at least 48 hours prior to the scheduled presentation slot.

5. Refund Claim Process

To initiate a refund request, send an email to the Conference Secretariat containing:

  • Full delegate name and registered email address.
  • Transaction ID / Invoice reference number.
  • Brief reason for cancellation or substitution request.

Refunds are credited back directly to the original payment source (Credit Card, Debit Card, UPI, or Bank Account) within 5 to 7 working days of approval.

For billing inquiries or cancellation requests:
conferences@scholarvault.in
