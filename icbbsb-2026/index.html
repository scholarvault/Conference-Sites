Six Specialized Research Tracks

From deep generative protein models to commercial precision fermentation, explore multidisciplinary research accelerating the global circular bioeconomy.

TRACK 01

Bio-AI Applications & Protein Engineering

Harnessing foundation models and machine learning for generative biology, molecular docking, and structural bioinformatics.

  • Deep learning for protein folding & design
  • Genomic foundation models & gene expression
  • AI-accelerated computational drug discovery
  • De novo enzyme engineering & catalytic optimization

TRACK 02

Bioeconomy Models, Policy & LCA

Economic frameworks, carbon accounting, and strategic roadmaps for transitioning to sustainable bio-based production systems.

  • Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) & carbon accounting
  • Biomass supply chain optimization
  • National bioeconomy roadmaps & regulation
  • Techno-economic analysis (TEA) of bio-plants

TRACK 03

Sustainable Biomanufacturing & Fermentation

Scalable precision fermentation, microbial cell factories, and advanced bio-foundry infrastructure.

  • Microbial cell factory optimization
  • Precision fermentation scale-up engineering
  • Cell-free protein synthesis & automation
  • Green downstream purification & recovery
Track Scope →
TRACK 04

Synthetic Biology & AI Gene Networks

Computational biology and synthetic circuitry engineering for industrial micro-organisms and therapeutic platforms.

  • Synthetic gene circuit modeling & logic gates
  • CRISPR off-target prediction via deep learning
  • Genome-scale metabolic network reconstruction
  • Automated strain design & bio-foundries

TRACK 05

Green Bioprocessing & Bioreactor Systems

Innovations in smart bioreactor design, continuous bioprocessing, non-food feedstocks, and industrial scale biopolymers.

  • Smart bioreactor monitoring via PAT & digital twins
  • Continuous bioprocessing & perfusion systems
  • Non-food agricultural & forestry feedstocks
  • Bio-based PHA, PLA, and biopolymer synthesis

TRACK 06

Circular Bioeconomy & Waste Valorization

Transforming agricultural residues, food waste, and gaseous carbon into high-value bio-chemicals and sustainable materials.

  • Microbial enzymatic plastic biodegradation
  • Industrial CO2 & syngas bio-fixation
  • Bio-fertilizers, bio-char & mineral recovery
