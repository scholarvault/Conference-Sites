Academic Submissions

Call for Papers & Contributions

We invite original theoretical, empirical, and applied engineering contributions across our six multidisciplinary Bio-AI, precision fermentation, and circular bioeconomy tracks.

Track Taxonomy

Explore Conference Tracks

Authors may submit 250–500 word abstracts aligned with one or more of the following scientific research domains.

TRACK 01

Bio-AI Applications & Protein Engineering

Focuses on foundational AI architectures and deep generative neural networks applied to macromolecular modeling, enzyme design, and structural biology.

  • Diffusion and transformer models for de novo protein backbone generation
  • Large genomic foundation models & regulatory sequence prediction
  • AI-guided directed evolution and high-throughput screening
  • Molecular dynamics acceleration via physics-informed neural networks (PINNs)
TRACK 02

Bioeconomy Models, Policy & LCA

Encompasses macro-economic modeling, carbon life-cycle assessment (LCA), techno-economic analysis (TEA), and policy frameworks for bio-based industrial scaling.

  • Cradle-to-grave Life Cycle Assessment for bio-manufactured goods
  • Biomass logistics and agricultural feedstock supply chain optimization
  • National bioeconomy strategies, carbon credit verification & ESG reporting
  • Comparative techno-economic analysis of bio-refineries vs petrochemical plants
TRACK 03

Sustainable Biomanufacturing & Precision Fermentation

Addresses the engineering bottlenecks of scaling precision fermentation from bench-scale shake flasks to industrial multi-thousand-liter bioreactor tanks.

  • Engineering host chassis (yeast, fungi, bacteria) for high-titer production
  • Gas-fermentation platforms (methanotrophs, acetogens)
  • Cell-free continuous biomanufacturing and enzymatic synthesis
  • Green downstream extraction, filtration, and product crystallization
TRACK 04

Synthetic Biology & AI-Driven Gene Networks

Covers synthetic biology circuitry, genetic reprogramming, metabolic flux optimization, and automated strain engineering via bio-foundry robotics.

  • Computationally designed synthetic gene switches and feedback loops
  • Machine learning models for CRISPR guide RNA efficiency and off-target avoidance
  • Genome-scale metabolic models (GEMs) and 13C metabolic flux analysis
  • Laboratory automation, high-throughput liquid handling & cloud laboratories
TRACK 05

Green Bioprocessing & Bioreactor Systems

Innovations in bioreactor physics, real-time sensing, non-food feedstocks, and biodegradable polymer synthesis.

  • Process Analytical Technology (PAT) and real-time Raman spectroscopy
  • Hydrodynamics, mass transfer, and CFD modeling in large-scale vessels
  • Lignocellulosic, algal, and industrial wastewater feedstocks
  • Industrial synthesis of PHAs, bio-based polyamides, and bio-lubricants
TRACK 06

Circular Bioeconomy & Waste Valorization

Converting organic sidestreams, municipal food waste, agricultural residues, and greenhouse emissions into valuable resources.

  • Enzymatic recycling and microbial degradation of post-consumer plastics
  • Microbial protein and single-cell oils from agri-food sidestreams
  • Biological carbon capture, utilization, and storage (Bio-CCUS)
  • Bio-mining, rare earth element bio-recovery & soil bio-remediation
Author Guidelines

Submission Standards & Format

All contributions must be original, previously unpublished work adhering to international research ethics standards.

Abstract Structure (250–500 Words)

Abstracts must clearly outline: 1) Background & Problem, 2) Methodology / AI Architecture, 3) Key Experimental or Computational Results, and 4) Broader Significance to Bioeconomy/Biomanufacturing. Include 4–6 relevant keywords.

Rapid Review & Quality Metrics

Abstract submissions are screened by our technical program committee within 3–5 working days. Evaluations prioritize scientific novelty, clarity of metrics, reproducible data methods, and track alignment.

Presentation Format

All accepted contributions are presented as Virtual Research Presentations (15-minute live broadcast + 5-minute interactive Q&A). Presenting authors receive an official Certificate of Presentation and proceedings indexation.
