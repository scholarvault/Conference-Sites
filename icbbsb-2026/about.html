Summit Profile

About ICBBSB 2026

Accelerating the convergence of computational artificial intelligence, metabolic engineering, and sustainable biomanufacturing to power a regenerative global bioeconomy.

Conference Vision

Engineering Biology for a Decarbonized Future

The global industrial complex faces an urgent mandate: transition away from petrochemical synthesis toward sustainable, bio-based alternatives.

The International Conference on Bio-AI, Bioeconomy & Sustainable Biomanufacturing (ICBBSB 2026) serves as the premier global forum bridging computational biology and physical manufacturing. By bringing together AI foundation modelers, synthetic geneticists, chemical engineers, and environmental economists, the conference addresses the core engineering bottlenecks in scale-up, metabolic pathway design, and carbon accounting.

Conducted 100% virtually on 18–19 December 2026, ICBBSB 2026 eliminates geographical barriers, fostering high-signal scientific collaboration across academia, government bio-foundries, and commercial biotech scale-ups.

Pillars of Impact

Core Scientific Objectives

  • Bio-AI Optimization: Benchmark machine learning architectures for enzyme design and metabolic flux.
  • Circular Valorization: Develop low-cost methods for transforming agricultural residues and CO2 into value-added polymers.
  • Bioprocess Scale-Up: Solve oxygen transfer, mixing, and continuous downstream purification bottlenecks in precision fermentation.
  • Transparent Peer Review: Provide authors with 3–5 day turnaround, constructive reviews, and permanent ISBN indexing.
