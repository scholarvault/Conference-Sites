Six Conference Tracks

  1. Bio-AI Applications & Protein Engineering: Diffusion & transformer models for de novo enzyme synthesis and genomic prediction.
  2. Bioeconomy Models, Policy & LCA: Carbon accounting, biomass supply chains, and techno-economic modeling.
  3. Sustainable Biomanufacturing & Precision Fermentation: Scale-up engineering, continuous bioprocessing, and cell-free synthesis.
  4. Synthetic Biology & AI-Driven Gene Networks: Synthetic gene circuitry, CRISPR off-target prediction, and automated bio-foundries.
  5. Green Bioprocessing & Bioreactor Systems: Smart bioreactors, PAT digital twins, and biodegradable PHA/PLA polymer synthesis.
  6. Circular Bioeconomy & Waste Valorization: Agri-food waste valorization, enzymatic plastic degradation, and CO2 bio-fixation.
