Common Queries

Agenda FAQ

When will I receive my certificate?

Digital certificates for participation and presentation will be emailed to all registered attendees within 7-10 business days after the conclusion of the conference.

Will the sessions be recorded?

Yes! All keynote and technical sessions are recorded. Registered participants will get exclusive access to the recordings via the app.scholarvault.in Dashboard within 7 working days.

How do I access the Zoom link?

A secure Zoom meeting link and password will be sent to your registered email 24 hours prior to the conference via a personal invite.

What time zone is the schedule in?

The conference runs on Indian Standard Time (IST, UTC +5:30). Your local time is automatically detected and displayed alongside each session for your convenience.
